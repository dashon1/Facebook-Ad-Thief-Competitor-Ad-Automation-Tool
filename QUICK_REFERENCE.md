## ⚡ Quick Start (2 Minutes)

1. **Open**: https://ad-thief.pages.dev/new
2. **URL**: `https://www.facebook.com/nike/`
3. **Brand**: `Your Brand Name`
4. **Upload**: Your product image (PNG/JPG, <10MB)
5. **Settings**: Max Ads: `5`, Batch Size: `2`
6. **Click**: "Generate Inspired Creatives"
7. **Wait**: 3-5 minutes
8. **Download**: Your branded ads!

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Stuck in "Queued"** | Wait 3 min, then refresh |
| **No ads found** | Check URL format (use Page URL) |
| **Prohibited content** | Normal (10-20%), try more ads |
| **Upload fails** | Check size (<10MB) and format (PNG/JPG) |
| **Slow progress** | Normal, wait up to 10 min for large jobs |

## 📋 Product Image Best Practices

**Requirements**:
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Size: Under 10MB
- Resolution: 1080x1080px or higher

**Best Results**:
- ✅ Clean/transparent background
- ✅ Professional lighting
- ✅ High resolution
- ✅ Sharp focus

**Tools**:
- Remove bg: remove.bg
- Resize: canva.com
- Compress: tinypng.com
